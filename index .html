Signs your lighting is worth a second look.

Most buildings don’t have a lighting problem. They have a run-time, maintenance or record-keeping problem that shows up as one.

Get a free lighting savings estimate

  • The common areas look almost the same at 2:00am as they do at the busiest part of the evening.

  • Ageing fluorescent fittings, or a patchwork of replacements with mismatched colour and brightness.

  • The same failures keep bringing you back: drivers, LED strip, flickering fittings.

  • Nothing on file to show a committee or inspector that emergency and exit lighting has been tested.

LED is only half the story.

The fitting reduces how much electricity the light uses. The controls reduce how long you pay for it.

  • Occupancy

    Switch or dim lighting when areas are empty.

  • Dimming

    Keep useful background light without paying for 100% output.

  • Timing

    Stop exterior and common-area lights running longer than required.

What a building manager should expect.

  • Lower running costs

    Sensors, dimming and timers reduce the hours you pay for, not just the watts.

  • Consistent light quality

    Matched colour temperature and even coverage across corridors, car parks and common areas.

  • Fewer repeat callouts

    Better fittings and correctly specified drivers mean less time chasing failures.

  • Records you can file

    Emergency and exit lighting tested to the relevant Australian Standard and documented for your committee or inspector.

We map circuits and check load before we touch a fitting, and work after hours where needed to keep disruption to tenants and residents low.
